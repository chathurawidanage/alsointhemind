Chapter 10

Find a Way to Bring Many
to This Path
(July 2001)

14 July 2001

x [Personal Note:] All blessings for creating the opportunity for many to follow the Noble path. (Regarding a separate sīmā and the safeguarding of purity of the Saṅgha).2

x To do everything with the advice, blessings and approval of [Ven. Madihe Paññāsīha] Mahānāyaka Thera.

x Create separate cārikā bhikkhus.3

x Many will get an impression in the future from what you do in the morning (piṇḍapāta) and some will want to join.

x Kasibhāradvāja Sutta (SN7.11; Kasi Bhāradvāja) applies especially to you: when you happen to discourse to others, you must discourse to them from [within] their [own] framework or language, your idea or Dhamma ideas it will create an impression.

x You must find a way to bring many to this path.

x Create separate cārikā piṇḍapāta bhikkhus.

x (Bhikkhus in mind) recruit young ones from the age 20 who are especially coming from good families, who can make a determination on the essential goal.

  1. 2 Ven. Gnanaloka Thera expressed this statement of gratitude to his teacher, Venerable Ñāṇavimala Mahāthera, on the day the new sīmā was opened at Parappaduwa Island Hermitage. A sīmā is the legally defined boundary within which the Bhikkhu Saṅgha (Monastic Community) conducts formal communal acts—such as higher ordination (upasampadā), the closing of the rains-retreat, disciplinary procedures, and the annual robe-offering ceremony (kaṭhina) to safeguard procedural purity.

  2. 3 Ven. Ñāṇavimala encouraged the establishment of such monks to preserve the ancient forest tradition of renunciation and simplicity. Interestingly, this advice was given to a young monk—Ven. Gnanaloka—who had been in robes for only two years at the time.
